## D-070 · 2026-10-19 · Stage 10.1: FIM uses log|I| at non-zero bias only, with Nelder–Mead refinement
The Fisher-matrix study uses the observable `ln|I|` at the four **non-zero** bias points `[-50, -25, 25, 50]` mV (the `V=0` point carries no current information and is excluded), forming `J = ∂ ln|I| / ∂ ln(R, |q_w|)` and `F = JᵀJ`. The inverse uses a batched 30×30 grid cost followed by a Nelder–Mead refinement from the top-4 candidates (`xatol=1e-6, fatol=1e-12, maxiter=300`). Rationale: the dimensionless sensitivities are O(1) for radius and O(1e-4) for charge, so a log-log parameterization and a local refinement are needed to resolve the near-null directions.
