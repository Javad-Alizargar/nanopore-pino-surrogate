## 1. Research question

Can a **single parameter-conditioned physics-informed neural operator** replace repeated numerical solution of the 2D axisymmetric Poisson–Nernst–Planck (PNP) system across a continuous 4D operating space, while preserving boundary-layer (Debye-layer) accuracy and charge conservation that a fixed-state surrogate cannot provide?

Concretely: learn an operator
$$
\mathcal{G}_\theta : \boldsymbol{\mu} = (V_{\text{app}},\, C_0,\, q_w,\, R) \;\longmapsto\; \big(\psi(r,z),\, c_+(r,z),\, c_-(r,z)\big)
$$
for a solid-state nanopore of radius $R$ and fixed length, subject to the PNP equations, such that one trained operator serves the whole operating space rather than one trained network per state.

## 2. Context and motivation

The preceding study (COMPHY-D-26-00857; `Manuscript_clean.docx`) established an **exact-reservoir-boundary, data-assimilated PINN (DA-PINN)** anchored to a conservative Scharfetter–Gummel finite-volume (SG/FV) reference for charged nanopore biointerfaces. That work is scientifically solid but explicitly **fixed-state**: each network interpolates one operating point. Its own limitations section disclaims parameter generalization.

Two obstacles identified there define this project:

1. **Boundary conditions dominate the physics.** Reservoir and charged-wall conditions strongly constrain the solution; treating them weakly degrades accuracy. An operator must build them in by construction.
2. **Catastrophic cancellation in net charge.** The Poisson source is the net charge density $\rho = c_+ - c_-$, a small difference of two large, nearly equal fields. A surrogate that predicts $c_+$ and $c_-$ independently inherits a large relative error in $\rho$, corrupting the electrostatic solve.

## 3. Proposed contribution

Upgrade from a **fixed-state DA-PINN** to a **Parameter-Conditioned Physics-Informed Neural Operator (PIO)** combining an **Exact-Boundary Fourier Neural Operator (FNO)** and a **PI-DeepONet**, for 2D axisymmetric PNP transport across the 4D operating space
$$
\boldsymbol{\mu} = (V_{\text{app}},\, C_0,\, q_w,\, R).
$$

### Core innovations

**(a) Exact-reservoir boundary operator ansatz.**
Enforce reservoir (Dirichlet) and charged-wall boundary conditions *by construction* inside the operator (hard-constrained output transforms / distance-weighted lifts), extending the exact-boundary philosophy of the DA-PINN to a parametric operator, so boundary accuracy does not depend on penalty weights.

**(b) Charge-balanced output reparameterization.**
Predict the solution in a reparameterized basis that removes the cancellation: the electrostatic potential $\psi$, the **total salt** $s = c_+ + c_-$, the **net charge** $\rho = c_+ - c_-$ (predicted directly rather than by subtraction), and a **local electroneutrality / electrochemical-potential comparison** term. Recover $c_\pm = (s \pm \rho)/2$ only at evaluation. This is the key numerical-conditioning innovation: the quantity that feeds Poisson ($\rho$) is a first-class network output, not a difference of two noisy large numbers.

**(c) Held-out interpolation vs. Out-of-Distribution (OOD) extrapolation across Debye-layer regimes.**
Systematically evaluate the trained operator on (i) interpolated operating points inside the training envelope and (ii) OOD extrema crossing the Debye length $\lambda_D \propto C_0^{-1/2}$ regimes (thin ↔ thick double layers, low ↔ high $q_w$, small ↔ large $R$). Report where the operator fails and why — an honest generalization map rather than a single aggregate error.

**(d) Amortized computational break-even analysis.**
Compare the operator's inference cost against the verified SG/FV reference (baseline stated as **≈ 0.44 s per solve**; to be re-measured on this Apple-M1 machine) and quantify the **amortization break-even** $N^\*$: the number of operating points at which (training + inference of $N$ points) beats $N$ direct SG/FV solves. Include the DA-PINN and cached-interpolation comparators.

**(e) Interactive browser-based visual simulator and dual GitHub repositories.**
- Browser simulator: export the operator to ONNX and run client-side (ONNX Runtime Web / JS) with a Python app (gradio/streamlit) for exploration of the 4D space.
- `reproducibility/github/` — **private full repository** (private unless explicitly authorized otherwise).
- `reproducibility/public_repo/` — **minimal, clean, shareable** reproducibility package (allowlist-built; no secrets, no restricted data).

## 4. Hypotheses

- **H1 (interpolation):** the parameter-conditioned operator attains relative $L_2$ error of the same order as the fixed-state DA-PINN on held-out interpolated states.
- **H2 (OOD):** accuracy degrades predictably with distance from the training envelope and as the Debye length leaves the sampled range; failure modes are systematic, not catastrophic.
- **H3 (conditioning):** the charge-balanced reparameterization (b) materially reduces relative error in $\rho$ versus predicting $c_+, c_-$ independently.
- **H4 (amortization):** a finite, practically reachable break-even $N^\*$ exists versus the SG/FV solver.

Negative results (e.g., no reachable break-even, or OOD failure) are valid outcomes and will be reported.
